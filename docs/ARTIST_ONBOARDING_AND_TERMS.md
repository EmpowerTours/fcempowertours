# Publish your music on EmpowerTours

*Last updated: 19 August 2026 · Questions: admin@empowertours.xyz*

---

## How it works

EmpowerTours is a home for independent music. You publish your own tracks, you keep
ownership, and you reach listeners directly.

1. **Upload your track.** It's registered on-chain as a Music NFT with your name on
   it. You stay the owner.
2. **It goes on the radio.** Your track joins the shuffle on EmpowerTours Radio.
   It's free to listen on the EmpowerTours app and website. In the Farcaster mini
   app the radio is part of a subscription — those subscribers are what fund the
   artist pool.
3. **You get paid from the pool.** Subscribers pay into a monthly pool, split
   between artists by how much each was played. More plays, bigger slice.
4. **You can earn more directly.** Fans can license your track, bump it up the
   queue, or run a paid shout-out. Those payments are shared with you on-chain.

Nothing here is exclusive. Release your music wherever else you like.

## What you're agreeing to

**It's yours to share.** You made it, or you have the rights to put it here —
including samples, features, and cover art. If someone else needs to be paid or
credited, that's on you to sort out before you upload.

**You let us stream it.** You're giving us permission to host, stream, cache, and
re-encode your track and its artwork across our apps and website so people can
listen, and to use the track name and cover art to promote it. That's all this
permission is for. It's non-exclusive and it ends when you take your music down —
except for backups, logs, and anything already written on-chain, which we can't
reach in.

**You keep ownership.** None of this transfers your copyright.

**We show your name.** Your artist name, Farcaster handle if you have one, wallet
address, and cover art get displayed alongside your music.

**Payouts depend on plays.** Your share is your plays divided by all plays that
month, times the pool, after the platform and reserve cuts. It's not a fixed
amount and we can't promise a figure. Paid on-chain to your wallet. Your taxes are
yours.

**Fake plays don't count.** Botted or manipulated plays get excluded, and we can
withhold or claw back a payout tied to fraud.

**Don't upload things you shouldn't.** Nothing that infringes someone's rights, is
illegal, or is built to attack people or systems. We'll take down content that
breaks this.

**You can leave whenever.** Pull your music any time and we'll stop streaming it.
We can also take content down for a valid legal complaint or repeat infringement.
Payouts you already earned before leaving are still yours.

**If the splits change, we'll say so first,** and changes only apply going forward.

**As-is.** We run this as well as we can, but we're not liable if an indexer, the
network, or a third party breaks and costs you a payout.

**If your upload gets us sued** over rights you didn't actually have, that's on
you.

This agreement is between you and **Empowertours S.A.S. de C.V.**, and Mexican law
applies.

## Copyright complaints

If you think something on EmpowerTours infringes your copyright, email
**admin@empowertours.xyz** with the subject "Copyright Notice" and include:

1. Your signature (typing your name is fine).
2. What work of yours was infringed.
3. Which track on EmpowerTours you're talking about — name and token ID is ideal,
   anything that lets us find it works.
4. Your name, address, and email.
5. A statement that you believe in good faith this use wasn't authorised.
6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the above is accurate and you're the
   rights holder or acting for them.

We review every valid notice and may remove or block the track. We may pass your
notice, contact details included, to the artist who posted it.

**If we took your track down and you think we got it wrong,** email the same
address with: what was removed and where it was; a statement under penalty of
perjury that you believe it was removed by mistake; and your name, contact details,
and agreement to settle any dispute in Mexico.

Artists who pile up valid infringement notices get suspended.
